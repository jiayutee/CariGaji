## 4. Objection cheat-sheet (honest answers)

- **"Is pay guaranteed?"** — No, and we won't say it is. The wage and hours are in a contract both sides sign, hours are QR-timestamped, and during the pilot the owner personally handles payment and settles any dispute within 48h (§5).
- **"Why not just use my WhatsApp group?"** — Keep it if it works. Here the rate is agreed in writing before the shift and check-in is timestamped, so no-shows have a record and nobody argues about hours.
- **"What does it cost?"** — Free for two months from your first posted shift, then 15% on top of the wage. The worker gets their full agreed rate.
- **"What if the worker doesn't show?"** — Reliability scores and strikes follow the worker across the platform. We do not compensate you for a no-show; saying otherwise would be overselling.
- **"Who employs the worker?"** — You engage the worker directly; CariGaji is the marketplace and provides the contract and records. For anything beyond that, refer to the T&C — don't improvise (§5).
- **"What if I cancel late?"** (ask it before they do) — Inside 24h you owe each confirmed worker 50% of the signed wage, or 100% if they turn up. Computed from the contract, not negotiated.
